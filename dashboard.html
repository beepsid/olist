How the data was prepared

  • 9 relational CSVs joined into a star schema: 2 fact tables (orders, order items), role-playing geography dimensions for customers and sellers, and a custom DAX calendar.
  • 14 data-quality issues logged and resolved, with no real revenue rows dropped. Examples: 610 products with no category were kept as "Uncategorized"; 814 duplicate reviews were deduplicated to the latest review per order; 261k duplicate geolocation rows were collapsed to zip-code centroids; zip codes were re-padded to stop silent join failures.
  • Definitions: revenue = item price on delivered orders. Late = delivered after the estimated delivery date. Review = the latest review per order.
  • The full Power BI file has 5 pages and 20+ DAX measures (MoM growth, rolling 3-month revenue, on-time rate by seller and state). This page is an interactive web summary of it.
